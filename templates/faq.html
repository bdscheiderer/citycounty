Frequently Asked Questions

A recent bill passed by the Missouri General Assembly used the following language to describe a county:

"Any county with more than forty thousand but fewer than fifty thousand inhabitants and with a county seat with more than fourteen thousand but fewer than eighteen thousand inhabitants."

To determine which county fits this description, a search through the population tables located in the Missouri Official Manual (the Blue Book) would be necessary. A diligent search would narrow this down to Butler County (County seat: Poplar Bluff). This form of statutory language is used to avoid drafting a “special law” which is generally prohibited by the Missouri Constitution.

This website is an online resource to expedite that search process.

Official population data comes from the U.S. Decennial Census. Specific Missouri population data is published by the Missouri Secretary of State in the Official Manual (the Blue Book), and the Office of Missouri State Demographer. City and county classification data is also published in the Missouri Official Manual.

Missouri counties are grouped into classes. This search uses the classes published in the Missouri Official Manual (the Blue Book). They are the same labels in the Classification menu on the home page:

  • Charter
  • First class
  • Second class
  • Third class with township
  • Third class without township
  • Fourth class

For the rules that decide a county's class, see the Blue Book and the Missouri statutes.

Missouri cities are grouped into classes. This search uses the classes published in the Missouri Official Manual (the Blue Book). They are the same labels in the Classification menu on the home page:

  • Home rule
  • Third class
  • Fourth class
  • Special charter
  • Village

The search also includes census-designated places. The U.S. Census Bureau names those places for statistics. A few very small places in the database have no class listed. Leave Classification blank to include them. For the rules that decide a city's class, see the Blue Book and the Missouri statutes.
